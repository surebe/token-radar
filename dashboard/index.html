How to read this page, and what the data cannot say

The odds are a prevalence fit, not a prophecy: the fit learns how often protocols with these figures already record a token, which mixes "will launch" with "already did" — the snapshot holds no launch dates, so no rung of the ladder is a timing claim. "Records no token" is made precise as both token fields (CoinGecko id and symbol) empty; 258 protocols carry exactly one of the two, so a one-field rule misreads the population in both directions. Listing dates are missing for 113 protocols, two of them on the watchlist; their ages are imputed at the population median and flagged on their rows. The value fit learns from the 128 traded tokens that record a market cap; the other 288 launched protocols record none, so the fit describes protocols that report a cap, not all that trade. Seven-day growth is winsorized to [−50%, +100%] because the raw field runs to +4,448%. The verdicts' one scenario is printed above the board; change it and every verdict on the page moves with it, while board.json keeps the canonical one. Nothing here is financial advice.
